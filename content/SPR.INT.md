Regular Gameplay: Acts like rush or the Angler, flickering lights before rushing through the rooms, killing anyone in its path unless they are out of view or in a hiding spot

Boss: Will dash rapidly through the room, bouncing off of walls, and shoots fast projectiles rapidly, will pause through the fight, allowing you to shoot it

Story Mechanics: It is a program that rapidly goes through the files, destroying anything that is not supposed to be there, is very fast at going through files, but is not very good at deleting files, so will more often than not miss files or programs that are not supposed to be there if they are in a sub-file or are hidden well enough from the program
